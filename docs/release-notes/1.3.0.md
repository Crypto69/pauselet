## What's new in 1.3.0

Four things a tester asked for after 1.2.1, all about the Critical
takeover and the History tab.

**A reminder can be silent.** The Sound list in a reminder's editor now has
a **None** row beside Default and the named sounds. A reminder set to None
makes no sound when it fires and no chime when its timer ends, even while
"Play sounds" is on in Settings. The warning before a takeover keeps its own
sound switch, and the voice coach its own setting. On iPhone, where the Lock
Screen alarm cannot be made silent outright, Pauselet gives it a silent
sound file instead; the editor says so under the picker.

**The takeover can finish by itself.** Under a Critical reminder's countdown
there is a new **Finish automatically when the timer ends** switch. When the
ring reaches zero the takeover shows "complete", plays its chime, and about a
second and a half later closes as if you had pressed Done — recorded in
History as completed. If an exercise is being coached at that moment it waits
for you instead.

**The takeover can hold the screen.** Also under the countdown, **Can be
dismissed** offers three levels: *Any time*, which is how every takeover has
always behaved; *Only snoozed until the timer ends*, where Done waits for the
ring but Snooze still works so a phone call has an exit and the reminder
comes back; and *Not until the timer ends*, where both buttons and their keys
wait. On the Mac and Windows a locked takeover can still be left in an
emergency by holding Esc for five seconds, which History records as
dismissed. On iPhone, leaving the app is always possible; the reminder stays
on the Lock Screen, without a Done button, until the timer ends. Both
choices only appear once a countdown is on, and a preview is never held — its
hint says what the real takeover would do.

**History stops ticking.** The "when" column on the History tab used to count
up every second. It now says roughly when each thing happened and refreshes
once a minute; hover a row on the Mac for the exact time.

**Windows — the countdown keeps time through sleep.** The takeover's timer on
Windows counted ticks, so a PC that slept mid-activity finished late. It now
keeps a deadline, as the Mac and iPhone always did.

Reminders you already have are unchanged: everything here is off until you
turn it on, and a reminder left at its defaults is stored exactly as before.
