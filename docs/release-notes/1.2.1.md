## What's new in 1.2.1

**A quieter warning before a Critical takeover.** The warning that comes
before a Critical reminder takes over your screen can now be silent, or play
a sound you choose. In the reminder's editor, under Warning, turn off "Play a
sound with the warning" for a quiet card, or leave it on and pick a sound
under "Warning sound". On Windows, use the "Play a sound with the warning"
checkbox and the sound list beside "Listen". The takeover itself still plays
its own sound either way. Reminders you already have keep the warning sound
they had.

**Mac — time fields fixed.** The start and end times, the daily and weekly
"At" time and the quiet hours times now use the standard macOS time field.
Before, the colon could sit above the numbers, a field could shift while you
typed in it, and clicking the up or down arrows while typing left the old
number showing, which could then come back when you clicked away. Click the
hour or the minutes and type, or use the arrows.

**Mac — the menu bar panel opens where it should.** The panel under the
Pauselet icon sometimes opened with a gap below the menu bar, or with its top
row cut off. It now always opens directly under the icon, sized to fit your
reminders.

The quieter warning is the same on macOS, iPhone and Windows. The two fixes
are for the Mac only.
