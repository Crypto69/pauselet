## What's new in 1.1.0

**Every reminder wears its priority colour.** A reminder's icon now takes the
colour of its priority, the same as the dot beside it in the list: teal for
Subtle, blue for Normal, orange for Important, red for Critical. The colour
carries through to the reminder list, History, the corner cards and the
priority picker in the editor, so you can tell at a glance how much a
reminder matters before reading a word. A reminder that is switched off
stays grey.

**Critical reminders glow red at the edges.** The full-screen takeover keeps
its calm, dark look, with a faint red glow feathering in from the edges of
the screen to mark it as critical.

**Subtle is teal, not grey.** The Subtle dot was grey, which made those
reminders look switched off. It now matches the teal of the Subtle card.

The same on macOS, iPhone and Windows.
